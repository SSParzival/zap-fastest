## What is New in ZapFastest 0.31.0

### New Features

- **Send view-once photos from the composer:** Photos staged in the composer can now be toggled as view-once before sending. Refusals, connection drops, and composer switching preserve the view-once choice in the attachment draft.
- **Clear chat from the chat list context menu:** Added "Clear chat" directly to the chat list context menu, enabling one-click local and phone-synchronized message clearing without opening the chat info dialog.
- **Send messages from open locked conversations:** Locked conversations opened within the authenticated locked folder can now freely compose and send messages, respecting privacy boundaries without blocking outgoing dispatches.
- **System appearance default:** Newly linked accounts and fresh installations default to following the desktop environment's system appearance (light or dark mode) automatically.
- **Debian 12 release builds:** Linux release builds are compiled against Debian 12 (Bookworm) sysroots to ensure widespread glibc compatibility across Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch Linux, and derived distributions.

### Upstream Protocol & Synchronization Fixes

- **Pinned chats above parked drafts:** Pinned chats remain permanently at the very top of the chat list across all views, with parked unsent drafts ordered directly below pinned chats and ahead of recent activity. Typing within an open conversation preserves existing ordering until leaving.
- **Isolated composer Undo:** Keystroke Undo/Redo in the composer is strictly scoped to the active chat and active account, ensuring text from previous chats or accounts can never be restored across context switches.
- **Translucent custom themes on Linux:** Restored transparent window backings for see-through themes on Linux Wayland/X11 compositors, eliminating visual ghosting and window flicker.
- **Recipient-indexed group delivery receipts:** Receipts in group chats are now indexed by participant JID, dramatically speeding up receipt tracking in large groups.
- **Voice messages with duplicate first page:** Voice audio playback gracefully skips stray middle initial pages without refusing valid OGG/Opus voice notes.
- **Privacy-ID (@lid) chat merge preservation:** Message metadata, stars, pins, and motion clips survive correctly when privacy-id chats and phone-number chats merge.
- **Hidden HD photo and video copies:** WhatsApp's companion HD duplicate attachments are cleanly masked rather than appearing as confusing unsupported message tiles.
- **Display-off notification suppression:** Notifications are deferred to the phone while displays are off, preventing battery drain and notification clutter while away.
- **Large-window forwarding dialog:** Forwarding dialog now dynamically expands to give plenty of room on large displays while maintaining keyboard navigation.
- **Status mention notice filtering:** Status mention notices ("mentioned this group in their status") that have no chat representation are filtered out cleanly.
- **Atomic media confirmation:** Downloaded media paths are committed only after SQLite archive persistence succeeds, preventing orphaned active transfers upon storage errors.
- **Strict international phone number validation:** New contact creation verifies country codes and valid international phone digits, rejecting invalid alphanumeric entries.

### Retained ZapFastest Architecture & Power-User Ergonomics

- **True Multi-Account Isolation:** Independent session databases, credentials, SQLite encryption keys, and cache folders per linked WhatsApp account.
- **Durable SQLite Outgoing Queue:** All outgoing dispatches are safely staged in SQLite before network transmission, ensuring zero message loss across unexpected shutdowns or reconnects.
- **Custom Theme Catalog:** Native support for fastframe-theme and Omarchy dynamic JSON color palettes with real-time reload.
- **Native VoIP & Offline Transcription:** CPU-accelerated Whisper voice transcription running locally with zero network telemetry, alongside Sonora local echo cancellation.
- **Archive-Backed Gallery:** Instant search and inspection of media, links, and documents directly from the chat header.

## Known Limitations

- Group video calling repairs remain in development upstream; 1:1 voice and video calls continue using our stable native pipelines.
- Multi-account switching uses the top avatar switcher or keyboard shortcut `Ctrl+Shift+A`.
